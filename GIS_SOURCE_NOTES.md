
# CivicLens Delhi — GIS and Source Verification

## Scope
Delhi civic issue navigation for:
- Potholes and damaged roads
- Garbage and sanitation
- Broken streetlights
- Other civic grievances

## Official source register

### 1. Municipal Corporation of Delhi (MCD)
- Website: https://mcdonline.nic.in/
- Complaint information: https://mcdonline.nic.in/portal/feedback
- Official circular:
  https://mcdonline.nic.in/portal/downloadFile/circular_reg._complaints_lodged_on_mcd_311_app_230824113534834.pdf
- Verified from source:
  MCD describes its MCD 311 complaint channel. Its official
  circular lists potholes, non-removal of municipal solid waste,
  and non-functioning streetlights among reportable issues.
- Limitation:
  This does not establish that MCD is responsible for every
  location in Delhi.

### 2. New Delhi Municipal Council (NDMC)
- Website: https://www.ndmc.gov.in/
- Complaints: https://www.ndmc.gov.in/complaints.aspx
- Verified from source:
  The official page describes NDMC complaint channels and its
  311 mobile app.
- Limitation:
  Do not assume NDMC jurisdiction based only on a user entering
  "New Delhi". Verify the actual location and authority.

### 3. Delhi Public Grievance Monitoring System (PGMS)
- Website: https://pgms.delhi.gov.in/
- Verified from source:
  The portal describes a grievance system connected to
  Government of NCT of Delhi departments.
- Limitation:
  This is a general grievance route. Confirm scope and eligibility
  before recommending it for a particular municipal issue.

## GIS limitations
- Locality points in the initial map are approximate references.
- No official ward boundaries have been incorporated yet.
- No live civic incident dataset is included.
- A user-selected coordinate does not prove jurisdiction.
- No complaint is submitted by CivicLens.
